The platform

What a hydradermabrasion facial actually is

Most facials do one thing. This does nine, in sequence, on one machine — which means your practitioner can build the treatment around your skin rather than around whatever single device the room happens to contain.

A session typically combines deep cleansing and exfoliation, painless extraction, a targeted serum infusion, and a firming or brightening step. Nothing is injected, nothing peels, and you leave looking better rather than needing to hide for a week.

  • Built around your skin on the day — the sequence changes with what we find.
  • No needles, no chemical peel, no downtime — suitable before an event, not just after one.
  • TGA and FDA approved equipment — as with everything in the studio.
The nine modalities

Every handpiece, and what each one does

Your practitioner will not use all nine in one session. These are the tools available, and which ones you get depends entirely on what your skin needs.

01

Hydradermabrasion

A rotating tip deeply cleanses, exfoliates and extracts impurities while infusing one of three targeted solutions. Simultaneous suction lifts dead skin, oil and debris out of the pores.

02

Diamond Microdermabrasion

A diamond-tipped handpiece buffs away dead surface cells while suction removes debris and stimulates circulation — improving texture, reducing dullness and softening fine lines.

03

Ultrasound — Max & Precision

Sound-wave vibrations drive active ingredients deeper into the skin. The large tip covers cheeks and forehead; the precision tip works around the eyes, nose and mouth.

04

Sono-Extract

High-frequency vibration through a flat metal spatula loosens and lifts dirt, oil, dead cells and blackheads from the pores — deep cleansing with minimal irritation.

05

OxyGeno

Exfoliation, oxygenation and infusion in one step. It creates a CO₂-rich environment at the surface, triggering a natural oxygenation response, then infuses an O₂ OxyPro™ pod.

06

Rotating Multipolar RF

Controlled radiofrequency heats the dermal layers to stimulate collagen and elastin. The rotating head spreads energy evenly and adds a massage component that assists lymphatic drainage.

07

Bio-Lift Microcurrent

Low-level currents that mimic the body’s own electrical signals, stimulating facial muscles and skin cells to tone, lift, and support collagen and elastin production.

08

Nano Spray

Transforms carefully selected, professional-grade serums into an ultra-fine mist that penetrates deeply — boosting hydration and nutrient absorption without any irritation.

09

High Frequency

Mild electrical current generates oxygen-enriched molecules at the skin’s surface. It targets acne-causing bacteria, calms inflammation, stimulates circulation and supports healing.

Safety

When it is not appropriate

We complete a health screen before your first session. Any of the following means we either delay treatment or decline it — and we will tell you which, and why.

  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Skin cancer
  • Open lesions
  • Active herpes in the treatment area
  • Bacterial or fungal infections
  • Recent sunburn
  • Metal plates or pins
  • Warts
  • Certain medications, e.g. isotretinoin
  • Pacemakers or other medical devices
  • Suspicious pigmented lesions
  • Prescription-strength exfoliants (AHA, BHA, retinoids)

Before you come in

Stop prescription-strength exfoliants — retinoids, AHAs and BHAs — for several days beforehand, and avoid treatment on sunburnt skin. If you are on isotretinoin or have recently finished a course, tell us: there is usually a waiting period. If you are unsure about any medication you are taking, raise it at booking rather than on the day.
