The instrument

Thousands of micro-channels, precisely placed

The mesotherapy pen uses fine micro-needles to create thousands of micro-channels across the epidermis and dermis, letting serums, vitamins and other active ingredients penetrate far more effectively than a topical product ever could.

  • Dermoepidermal biostimulation — the pen itself stimulates collagen and elastin production, improving texture and firmness.
  • Targeted treatment — precise placement over wrinkles, fine lines or scars for more effective, localised results.
  • Comfortable — minimal discomfort and downtime, with little impact on your day.
  • Professional-grade — built to a standard that ensures consistent, compliant results.

Safety

When it is not appropriate

We complete a health screen before your first session. Any of the following means we either delay treatment or decline it — and we will tell you which, and why.

  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Active infection or open wounds in the area
  • Known allergy to any product ingredient
  • Bleeding disorders or blood thinners
  • Keloid scarring history
  • Autoimmune conditions under active treatment
  • Isotretinoin use within the last 6 months
  • Active cancer or current treatment

Sensitive to hyaluronic acid?

Say so at consultation. Miracle L is formulated without added hydration and is the product we reach for when HA-based blends aren't an option.
