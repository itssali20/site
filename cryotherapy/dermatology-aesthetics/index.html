The basics

What a cryo facial does to skin

Exposing skin to extreme cold and high pressure stimulates several natural physiological processes. Treatments help repair damaged tissue and support a smoother, tighter and healthier complexion.

  • Reduced swelling & redness — vasoconstriction settles puffiness and calms irritation.
  • Improved circulation — a fourfold increase in blood flow on rewarming, sustained four to six hours.
  • Increased collagen production — cold is a trigger for collagen synthesis.
  • Enhanced lymphatic drainage — less fluid retention, a more sculpted look.

Therapeutic effects

Four responses, and what each one is for

The same physiology that helps an injured knee does something rather different when it is applied to facial skin.

01

Reduced pain and inflammation

Blood vessels constrict immediately in response to extreme cold, temporarily reducing blood flow and alleviating swelling.

Nerve activity also decreases, which reduces sensations like itching and burning — highly effective for soothing the skin irritation caused by conditions such as acne.

02

Improved blood flow

On rewarming, vasodilation takes place and blood flow to the treated area increases around fourfold, delivering a much higher volume of oxygen and nutrients.

That boost in circulation lasts four to six hours post-treatment, resulting in a firmer and smoother complexion.

03

Enhanced skin repair

Collagen synthesis is triggered by extreme cold — the protein responsible for skin repair and elasticity.

New skin tissue forms at a faster, more efficient rate, supporting overall skin texture and reducing the appearance of fine lines, wrinkles, scars, blemishes and cellulite.

04

Efficient lymphatic drainage

Extreme cold combined with high pressure causes muscles in and near lymph vessels to contract, stimulating the body’s natural clearance system.

The result is more efficient removal of excess fluid and waste, reduced inflammation, less bloating and puffiness, and a tighter, more sculpted appearance.

An important distinction

CryoSculpting is not fat freezing

Both involve cold. Both aim at a more sculpted look. They work in completely different ways, and it matters that you know which one you are booking.

CryoSculpting (this page)Fat Freezing (cryolipolysis)
MechanismVasodilation and lymphatic drainageCryolipolysis — apoptosis of fat cells
Effect on fat cellsNone. They are unchanged.Destroyed, then cleared by the body
Temperature & time−78°C at the surface, seconds−4°C to −10°C, sustained 45 minutes
DeliveryHandheld CO₂ sprayVacuum applicator, 360° cooling
What changesWater retention, skin tightness, muscle definitionThickness of the fat layer itself
DurabilityTemporary — maintained with regular sessionsPermanent reduction in cell number

If your goal is a measurable reduction in a stubborn fat pocket, fat freezing is the treatment you want — read about cryolipolysis here. If your goal is tighter, less puffy, better-toned skin, CryoSculpting is the right tool.

Is it right for you?

Most healthy adults are suitable, and all skin types can be treated. Pregnancy and breastfeeding, cold sensitivities such as cold urticaria, impaired circulation or sensation, bleeding disorders, active infection or open wounds in the treatment area, and recent facial surgery or injectables can all make a treatment unsuitable or require a waiting period. We complete a health screen before your first session. Cryotherapy supports the management of skin conditions — it does not replace dermatological care, and we will say so if you should be seeing a doctor.
