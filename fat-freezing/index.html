The part most people don’t know

Dieting changes the size of your fat cells. It does not change how many you have.

This is the whole reason the treatment exists — and the reason a stubborn pocket keeps coming back no matter how disciplined you are.

Losing weight

Same cells, smaller

When you lose or gain weight, the volume of each fat cell changes. The number of cells stays exactly the same — which is why a pocket can shrink and still be there.

How it works

Four stages, over four to six weeks

The treatment itself is 45 minutes. What follows is your own body doing the clearing, which is why the result is gradual rather than immediate.

01

Targeted cooling

A vacuum applicator draws the tissue in and cools it with 360° coverage, held at a set temperature for the session.

02

Apoptosis

Fat cells are sensitive to cold in a way the surrounding skin, nerve and muscle tissue is not. The cooled fat cells begin to break down naturally.

03

Cleared by the body

Macrophages digest the cell debris, and the contents are carried away through the lymphatic system over the following weeks.

04

Processed as waste

The material travels to the liver, where it is processed and eliminated as waste through the body’s normal routes.

Dose is calculated, not guessed

Before treatment the area is measured with a scribing tool, and the temperature is set from the tissue thickness — a thinner area is treated differently from a thicker one. Anti-freeze membranes are applied for every session as standard, which is what prevents cold injury to the skin.

One device, two jobs

2 modalities, where most devices have 1

The EstelloV+ does not just cool. It combines cryolipolysis with radiofrequency skin tightening in the same system — most fat freezing devices on the market offer only the cooling side.

MODALITY 01

Fat freezing

Controlled cooling triggers apoptosis in targeted fat cells, which your body clears naturally over the following weeks — a genuine reduction in cell number, not just size.

MODALITY 02

Skin tightening

Radiofrequency built into the same handpiece supports collagen production in the treated area, helping the skin sit more firmly over the new contour rather than looking loose once the fat layer reduces.

Your session

What it actually feels like

Nobody enjoys surprises during a 45-minute treatment. Here is the honest sequence.

First

Warming

The area is pre-heated before cooling begins. It is comfortable, and it prepares the tissue.

Then

Suction, then cold

The applicator draws the tissue up — a firm pulling, pinching sensation — then cools. Expect intense cold, tingling, stinging, aching or cramping for the first few minutes. It subsides as the area goes numb, and most people read or work through the rest.

Straight after

Redness and firmness

The area will look red and feel firm, sometimes with mild bruising or blanching at the edges. Tingling and stinging as sensation returns is normal.

One to two weeks

Settling

Redness, bruising and swelling, along with tenderness, cramping or itching. Skin sensitivity, tingling and numbness can persist for several weeks. All of this is expected and temporary.

Results

Gradual, by design

Your body does the clearing, and it takes the time it takes. Anyone promising you a result next week is selling you something else.

4–6 WEEKS

Full effect

Most people see the full effect of a session within four to six weeks, as your body clears the treated fat cells.

PACKAGE OF 3

Best results

Treatments work best as a package of three sessions for a treated area, rather than a single visit.

4 WEEKS APART

Between sessions

Each session in a package needs at least four weeks before the next, so your body has time to clear the previous one first.

What it is not

Fat freezing is not a weight-loss treatment and it cannot replace liposuction. It is for reducing specific, stubborn pockets in people who are already near their target weight — and it works best alongside diet and exercise, not instead of them. It is not suitable if your BMI is over 30. A lasting result needs an appropriate number of sessions, a plan built around the shape you want, and lifestyle habits that hold it.

Suitability & safety

Read this part properly

This is the most involved treatment we offer, and it is not right for everyone. We would rather turn you away at consultation than treat you when we should not.

NOT SUITABLE

We will not treat you if

You have cryoglobulinemia or paroxysmal cold haemoglobinuria, or you are pregnant or breastfeeding. These are absolute — there is no version of the consultation where the answer changes.

NEEDS ASSESSMENT

Tell us about

Cold sensitivity such as cold urticaria · impaired circulation or sensation in the area · neuropathy · open or infected wounds · bleeding disorders or blood thinners · recent surgery or scar tissue in the area · eczema, psoriasis, dermatitis or rashes · hernia in the area · implanted devices such as pacemakers or defibrillators.

RARE EFFECTS

Uncommon, but you should know about them

Vasovagal symptoms — dizziness, nausea, flushing, sweating or fainting during or just after treatment. Late-onset pain — appearing several days after a session and resolving over several weeks. Subcutaneous induration — generalised hardness or discrete nodules in the treated area, typically resolving on their own within three to six months. Hyperpigmentation — usually resolving without intervention.

Paradoxical hyperplasia — a visible increase in tissue volume in the treated area, which may develop four to five months after treatment. It is rare, but surgical intervention may be required to correct it. We will talk you through this at consultation; it is not something we leave in fine print.

Not the same thing

Fat freezing is not CryoSculpting

We offer both, and they are regularly confused. One destroys fat cells; the other does not touch them.
